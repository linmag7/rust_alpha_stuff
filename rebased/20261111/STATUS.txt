aggressive GCC garbage collection, external calls and global-data relocations.

validation-only compatibility change; it is not a modification to fresh Rust.
Debug and release passed on QEMU and ES40: 315 layout/type checks, 2,370 constants,
ABI probes, memory mapping, pipes/errno, signals, termios, allocation, files,
